# Gorton Digital

Original, unmodified Regular and Heavy OpenType fonts by R. S. Bartgis,
from the [19 October 2017 release](https://github.com/drdnar/GortonDigital/releases/tag/20171019beta).
The font files retain their embedded copyright notice: copyright 2017 R. S. Bartgis.

The author permits noncommercial use. Commercial use requires a separate
license arranged with the author; see the [upstream licensing terms](https://github.com/drdnar/GortonDigital#licensing)
for contact information and the educational nonprofit donation arrangement.

Gorton Digital supplies the interface and Latin brush annotations. Characters
outside its coverage, including Japanese annotations and some keyboard symbols,
use the browser's fallback fonts.
